This project investigates whether Taiwan's domestic supply of used cooking oil could meet the country's own sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) targets. Drawing on USDA and Taiwanese government data, it models Taiwan's oil supply chain, current SAF production capacity, and emissions impact under three policy scenarios — from the status quo to full feedstock redirection — while running a Monte Carlo simulation to account for uncertainty in key assumptions. The findings show that Taiwan's committed SAF capacity falls well short of its 2030 goal, and that feedstock availability and refining capacity are both binding constraints, with different policy levers needed to address each.
